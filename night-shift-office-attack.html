WHY MICROGAMES?

GENRE · GAME DESIGN PRECEDENTS

ONE COMMAND. ONE PANIC.

Nintendo describes WarioWare's challenges as lasting seconds. The player learns a simple action immediately, then tries to execute it as the pace rises.

Melbourne's Dumb Ways to Die turned rail-safety messages into quick, absurd survival games. Its three-life structure helped shape our office-disaster format.

OUR RESPONSE Readable verbs, expressive failures, distinct interactions and two bosses. No upgrade menu between every game.

CONTEXT · CASUAL AND PORTABLE GAMING

GAMES BETWEEN THINGS.

Keogh and Richardson's research describes how casual mobile games fit around daily routines, waiting and short, flexible sessions. Play need not demand a whole evening.

Nintendo's WarioWare: Get It Together! supports handheld play. Portability and accessible controls make brief encounters a compelling design opportunity, not evidence of a proven genre comeback.

OUR RESPONSE One-click and drag interactions, short rounds, instant retries and a deliberate rest button. The player controls the break.
