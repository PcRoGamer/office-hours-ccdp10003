## Failures found by doing actual work

1. **End scene lacked visual consequence.** At 1920×1080 it showed a mostly empty dark stage with a large static CLOCK OUT sign. Implemented a Canvas2D epilogue in \`night-shift-v3/story/clockout.js\`: damaged QUOTA machine, damaged screen, expelled paper, animated debris, horrified Finch, relieved Mira, and a physical CLOCK OUT overprint. Both metrics still come from one frozen SYSTEMS report.
2. **Narrow receipt was functionally inaccessible.** Actual Chromium capture at 390×844 placed Flip, How Counted and Replay *below* the viewport. A screenshot revealed this even though prior layout checks were green. Now the receipt facts scroll independently while those three buttons remain visible at the bottom of the viewport. Re-rendered 390×844 and 1920×1080 screenshots confirm the difference.
3. **Keyboard focus disappeared on receipt re-render.** Flip via Enter, paper click and score-detail expansion now restore focus to the actual replacement control, not the destroyed DOM element.
4. **Promotion continuation race.** A stale 1.65-second timeout could call an old \`onContinue\` after a newer scene opened. The timer is now cancelled by \`hide/dispose/showReceipt/showPromotion\` and checks the original article identity before advancing.
5. **Actual SYSTEMS integration would have broken.** On the separate agent branch \`agents/v3-systems-g6-0811b\`, \`makeDraft\` and \`NightShiftRun.choosePerk\` use unprefixed IDs such as \`carbon-copy\`; STORY originally required \`perk:carbon-copy\`. STORY now accepts both IDs, looks up the correct display and upgrade level, and passes *the exact original input ID* to \`onPick\`.
6. **Real frozen scores were rejected by STORY.** SYSTEMS \`readScores\` returns \`officialScore\` / \`peopleScore\`, plus component objects in \`official\` / \`people\`, and facts such as \`bonusPacketOutcomes\`, \`frame\` and \`licenceTokensGiven\`. STORY originally only supported sample/legacy score fields. It now accepts the actual frozen \`readScores\` report or \`run.snapshot.endingSnapshot\`, reads its component breakdown, and uses its observed facts without inventing absent events.
7. **Promotion reactions lost SYSTEMS event evidence.** Live \`run.snapshot\` provides \`eventLedger\`, not always top-level worker/notice/frame/licence counts. Read-only \`observedRun\` now derives factual lines from only committed, unique events. Synthetic or aborted attempts never get credited as employee help.
