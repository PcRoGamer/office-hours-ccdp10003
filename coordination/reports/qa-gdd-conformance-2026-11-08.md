## Verdict: mechanical checklist largely met; experiential GDD NOT met

**Passing and genuinely implemented:**
- The eight first-run authored cards exist in prescribed order: I1/I3/S1/S2/P1/P4/T1/T4.
- Four promotion drafts and four stage-bounded directives are present, six perk IDs and real interactive physics scenes using locally bundled Planck 1.5.1.
- Real pointer-based CI covers all eight cards, boss through three phases, failure/retry, several perk builds and alternate T4 allocations.
- Ledger implements committed cards, unique packet IDs, two distinct scoring lenses, licence conservation, and freezes the final score. CI tested all 16 FILE/COOL boss packet masks.
- Responsive smoke tests exist and CI for feature SHA 4836908a succeeded. Do NOT equate automated tests with satisfying game feel or quality compliance.

**Direct misses / serious regressions:**
1. **Music and audio contract fails.** GDD §7 requires at least one engaging responsive music track with percussion layers for promotions/fight plus discrete SFX. `first-shift/game.js` implements only a short `AudioContext` oscillator+gain `sound(freq,dur)` helper and per-event beep combinations; there is no music player, no track or responsive musical layer. Existing Night Shift HD has a stronger integrated visual/audio identity than this bare beeping approach.
2. **Boss DOUBLE ENTRY directive is not per-phase as written.** GDD §4.3 demands one marked confirmation in *each boss phase*. `first-shift/boss.js` `create()` assigns `confirmed=phase!==2`; only phase index 2 requires confirmation. Rework this as meaningful three-phase rule/feeder interaction, not decorative text.
3. **Physical ending is incomplete.** GDD §6 requires a giant CLOCK OUT stamp physically crush THE QUOTA, gear 3 burst and a visible causal transformation into the stamp. `game.js` `finishBoss()` immediately switches `screen='ending'`, spawns four generic square module bodies with initial velocities and renders the receipt. This is a little physics confetti, not the climactic machine-crush interaction. No proper victory arc.
4. **HUD violates specified presentation.** GDD §6 and §7 require hearts and time in two tight corners during action, one imperative and a directive glyph. The implemented gameplay scenes display only a time badge + instructions and an always-on upper toolbar; there is no actual hearts/lives system, and research/mute/pause/motion text controls persist. The simplified scene resembles a graphic-design exercise rather than the agreed arcade UI.
5. **Art direction regressed vs Night Shift HD and has not satisfied the comparative quality gate.** Real Night Shift HD title has dramatic red/cream composition, expressive printer character, layered patterns, large purposeful typography, angled cinematic composition and dramatic diagonal elements. First Shift title has a plain cream card on striped background, uniformly small simplified character figures and a square machine face. Nearly every card uses the same pale mint header, beige ruled desk board, and immobile small Finch head. GDD §7 requires richer character states, differentiated stage language, camera scale, purposeful animation and stronger density than Night Shift HD. These are visibly not fulfilled, despite original procedural sprites existing.
6. **Portrait presentation contradicts the quality requirement.** In the actual 650x800 I1 screenshot the interactive canvas sits in roughly the upper half, the targets and instructions are materially shrunken, duplicated desktop/portrait headings appear, and the bottom ~300px is empty dark background. GDD explicitly requires readable targets/safe area at 650x800 and real screen-space composition; screenshots prove layout has not been craft-adjusted.
7. **Research integration too literal and intrusive.** At the bottom of I1, live gameplay says `Job Simulator asks: can the tool itself be fun? Feel the stamp land.`; promotion 1 adds `Brotato builds change combat...` and there is a fixed top-right RESEARCH button. This translates references into exposed explanatory prose rather than letting mechanics and fiction embody the question. The lecturer specifically criticised an explanatory conclusion in Project 1.2; GDD §8 requested concise optional context in natural intermissions. Need diegetic reasoning, source links optional rather than instructional commentary in every play scene.
8. **Pacing target not established; functional E2E appears drastically compressed.** GDD §1 and §2 target a first human clear of 4.5-6.5 minutes. Current CI full pointer path reports `wallMs=44314` (44.3 seconds) for eight cards + boss, and card committed elapsed times sum to only ~23.2 seconds. This is an intentionally automated optimal path, not a measured human playtime, but confirms CI did not validate authored full-run pacing or the target's dramatic breathing room. Do not claim user clear time or fun from that result.
9. **No real side-by-side rejection gate.** GDD §7, §9 explicitly require before/after visual reviews against V3/Night Shift HD and block promotion if it regresses. GitHub CI confirms correctness of pointer events/ledger, not superiority to incumbent in art, pacing, playfeel or sound. The evidence shows that an automated green build was allowed despite missed qualitative GDD contracts.
10. **Eight-card slice versus full v3 is a deliberate contraction.** The top-level master `NIGHT_SHIFT_GDD_V3_REFERENCE.md` specifies a 16-card catalogue, Printer Mutiny, more perks/directives, richer dramatic arc. The derived `FIRST_SHIFT_VERTICAL_SLICE_GDD.md` explicitly *cut* those to eight cards and one boss for the course release, and the Codex handoff called the slice authoritative. It is therefore inaccurate to blame Codex for omitting content that the handoff explicitly removed. The user's frustration about lost ambition implicates the scope decision itself. Reopen this deliberately if choosing a fresh full-game build; do not silently treat the slice as equivalent to the master GDD.

**Partial / fidelity concerns (do not oversell as entirely missing):**
- Physics is real Planck simulation for some props, including stamps, filing, coffee and boss projectiles; however visible scenes are mostly sparse icons on flat desk layouts, with little emergent toy clutter/room for experimentation. A solver alone did not produce Job Simulator-like delight.
- Perk draft preview animations exist in `perkPreview()` over ~1.5 seconds; do not claim they are absent. But tiny glyph previews on same static three cards are much less expressive than the demanded demonstrable in-world before/after effects.
- Character models and mood branches exist in `FSArt`, but real stage screenshots do not demonstrate strong multi-state expressions, facial performance or lively coworker consequences. Avoid asserting that all states are absent.
- Four directives and meaningful state/perk changes were implemented. Do not call the game mechanically nonexistent.
- In-game credits are present, and research question is explicitly stated. The critique is that their presentation over-explains rather than naturally demonstrating the argument; this is an artistic/academic distinction, not source total absence.

## Correct next production decision
**Do not promote First Shift over Night Shift HD.** Keep Night Shift HD untouched as playable and stylistic baseline. Use GDD v3 as creative master; use First Shift code as a *mechanics/ledger/Planck reference library*, not as the target art style. A clean new implementation can be justified, but **must include a pre-implementation comparative audit and actual progressive visual checkpoints**. If fresh Astra is assigned, require it to inspect both sets of real CI screenshots, explain what made Night Shift HD superior, declare what to salvage, then author and visibly review a vertical slice that outclasses it **before** widening into eight-card breadth. No claims of finished game until all visuals, music, interactive physics and full course work meet objective acceptance criteria.
