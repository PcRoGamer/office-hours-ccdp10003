All scenes emit `card-complete` at most once, via `context.events.emit`, with `cardId`, `outcome`, `evidence`, `attemptId`, and `synthetic:false`; bad actions use `card-failed`. No scene writes scores or mutates a shared ledger. The return object has `enter/update/draw/pointerDown/pointerMove/pointerUp/cancelPointer/snapshot/dispose`. Touch and pointer transforms are supplied by the preview; integrated transforms remain RENDER-owned.
