# First Shift release evidence

The isolated route is `first-shift.html`. Controls: click or tap to stamp and choose; drag to move objects and release to throw; Escape pauses. The toolbar contains research links, sound and reduced-motion controls.

Local checks on 8 November 2026: the clean-storage Playwright pointer run visited eight cards, four promotions, three boss phases, the ending and replay with no page errors (53.2 seconds automated wall time, not a first-time human duration). Failure/retry and pause were exercised. Opening interaction was checked at 1440×900 DPR 2, 1366×768, 1024×600 and 650×800. All 16 boss packet masks were tested via real pointer actions from a seeded pre-boss ledger, with zero perks; six actual licence allocation patterns conserved $80. The ledger suite passed seven tests. These targeted checks do not replace a first-time human playtest.

Evidence in `evidence/` includes frames and the recorded automated run. The portrait view retains unused space below the landscape playfield. The game has not been verified to meet the 4.5–6.5-minute first-time play target, and subjective enjoyment compared with prior releases has not been measured with a human player.
