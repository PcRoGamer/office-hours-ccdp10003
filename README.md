# OFFICE HOURS

A playable 3D office-survival game about gamified work, attention, advertisements and escalating corporate demands.

**Play:** https://pcrogamer.github.io/office-hours-ccdp10003/

**Screenshot gallery:** https://pcrogamer.github.io/office-hours-ccdp10003/gallery.html

**Full E2E and rubric audit:** https://pcrogamer.github.io/office-hours-ccdp10003/audit/

## The game
One click to clock in. AsterOS transitions from the physical 3D monitor into true native browser fullscreen, with a sharp, viewport-filling desktop and an Office View button to return to the room. Six reports, two mandatory policy decisions, two bosses, malware traps, shop upgrades, arcade diversions, and a replayable, decision-responsive ending. No forced research essay or tutorial.

## Controls
- Click CLOCK IN to enter native fullscreen. Use OFFICE VIEW in the taskbar or Escape to return to the 3D office; RESUME SHIFT re-enters. If browser fullscreen is blocked, AsterOS still fills the browser viewport.
- Postbox: open the latest assignment and its spreadsheet.
- Department Sheets: type 3*40 or =B2*C2 in the Total cell, then press Enter. Drag B2 across C2 to calculate D2, then drag the green D2 corner handle down to fill D3 and D4. You can also double-click the fill handle, press Ctrl+D, or use the guided FILL DOWN shortcut. Addition =B2+C2 and SUM(B2:C2) also work.
- Calculator: use the keypad or keyboard and insert the answer into the selected spreadsheet cell.
- Shop: spend credits on equipment, defence, healing and performance upgrades. Formula Auto-Pilot costs 85 credits and fills report formulas, not managerial policy choices; entering formulas and dragging are free.
- After completing Reports 4 and 5, choose how each accurate report is filed. Protecting employees costs credits and builds team backing. Hitting management targets earns credits but increases KPI pressure. These choices change the final boss and ending.
- Defend against the boss's telegraphed heavy attacks, or risk an OVERLOAD.
- The first email includes a brief 'why this game, why now' with research sources. Each report reward presents an in-context case note; later notes respond to advertising and policy choices while connecting workplace gamification to game-design scholarship. Personnel Files and Help contain optional deeper evidence.
- Every intrusive ad can be ignored for focus score or opened for a fight. Spam and ordinary email share the same Mark as Junk action; rewards and penalties appear after the choice.
- Click the speaker icon to mute/unmute. All music, UI cues, combat effects and ambience are original Web Audio synthesis.
- Save/progress persists in your browser. This game is desktop-first.

## Technical
Static React, React Three Fiber / Three.js, perspective-mapped in-world operating system, Vite build. No authentication, backend, media downloads or embedded paid assets.

## Academic context
Game-inspired response for the University of Melbourne's CCDP10003 Video Games: Remaking Reality, 2026 Project 2. This repository contains the deployable release and gameplay screenshots, not private university materials or submissions.
