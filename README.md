# OFFICE HOURS: QUOTA PANIC

**Play the current version:** https://pcrogamer.github.io/office-hours-ccdp10003/

Direct link: https://pcrogamer.github.io/office-hours-ccdp10003/arcade.html

Six tiny disasters. One very bad shift. Quota Panic is a fullscreen, pointer-driven 2D workplace arcade about gamification, attention, incentives, and who defines winning. It is the current submission candidate, **not** the older spreadsheet simulator or 3D office.

## How to play

Click **CLOCK IN**, then follow the large objective at the top of each stage. The six stages use deliberately different mechanics:

1. **Stamp Rush:** click good paperwork and leave spam unclicked.
2. **File Frenzy:** drag teal files left and coral files right.
3. **Printer Revenge:** repeatedly stamp the rogue printer, while dodging paper.
4. **Slide Showdown:** hold the pointer over verifiable evidence and avoid misleading charts.
5. **Recruitment Rumble:** hold the pointer and sweep across coworkers, not HR bots.
6. **The KPI Monster:** attack the multi-phase quota boss, respond to its telegraphed hazards and close intrusive popups.

Between stages, draft one of three power-ups. Every benefit changes your real run: focus, time, protection, speed, target size, team support, or corporate pressure. **Genuine workplace notifications** give useful aid, while scam popups punish careless acceptance. The game can be replayed, and its final boss retains checkpoints after setbacks.

At the end, choose **PEOPLE** or **KPI** to apply different fictional scoring rules to the *same completed shift*. Before choosing, no perspective is preselected. This is the game's central question: **who gets to decide what counts?**

**Controls:** mouse/touch pointer to click, drag, hover and hold. Esc pauses, M toggles synthesized game audio, F toggles browser fullscreen. The game is primarily designed for desktop browsers; no account or download is needed. On failure, retry the current stage rather than restarting the entire run.

## Critical and academic design

The pleasure of scoring, combos, bonuses, helpful notifications and upgrades is real gameplay, not an illustrative slideshow. The counterargument is also mechanical: management bonuses can raise the score while increasing pressure and distractions; team choices build mutual support but change what the scoreboard rewards. In the finale, changing the metric changes the apparent outcome without changing the work.

Each completed stage's upgrade screen briefly identifies the question raised by its mechanic. Optional **THE REAL RESEARCH** and **WHY THIS GAME?** panels link to the original cited game-design and workplace research. Fictional Aster Corp statistics and scoring formulas are *game rules*, not measurements of actual worker performance.

The project engages with gaming-culture critique, procedural rhetoric, game incentives, MDA, workplace gamification, attention and interruption. It is intended to be evaluated as a game first.

## Verification

The Quota Panic V3 release regression physically clicks, drags, follows charts and sweeps workers through all six stages in a real browser. It tests both people-led and KPI-led upgrade paths, the final scoring choice, no JS exceptions and the public entry route. The earlier 2D campaign remains separately tested.

Browser automation checks functionality and regression, **not** guaranteed enjoyment or an H1 grade. A genuine first-time human playtest is a separate evaluation.
