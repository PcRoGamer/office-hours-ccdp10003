Remaining limits and risks

  • Automated play proves behaviour, not first-time human enjoyment.
  • Some small labels may be difficult at narrow browser sizes or when projecting the 3D monitor.
  • No full screen-reader or WCAG audit has been performed; keyboard behaviour still needs human confirmation.
  • Six spreadsheet reports repeat a core action despite the changing tasks, shop, bosses and side quests.
  • No model can pre-certify a 100/100 grade. The course marker decides.
